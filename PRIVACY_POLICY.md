# Oct Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026


This Privacy Policy describes information that Oct Bot (the “Bot”) may process when used in Discord servers. It is written for the Bot's current feature set, which includes server utilities, moderation, music, AFK, welcome and role configuration, leveling, tickets, and virtual-economy features. Please verify the technical details in this policy against the deployed version before publishing.


## 1. Information that may be processed
Depending on which features a server uses, the Bot may process:

- **Discord identifiers and server configuration:** Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, and settings required to operate configured features.
- **Command and interaction information:** command names, options, and information users provide to commands. The Bot may access message content when needed to detect or respond to commands, depending on its configuration and Discord permissions/intents.
- **Moderation and utility data:** AFK status/messages, moderation command inputs, welcome settings, autorole/reaction-role configuration, and ticket configuration or ticket-related identifiers.
- **Progress and virtual-economy data:** experience, levels, virtual balances, inventory, and other game/progression records associated with Bot features.
- **Operational information:** error details and diagnostic information generated while the Bot runs. Whether IP addresses, request logs, or other telemetry are collected depends on hosting and service configuration.

The Bot is not intended to request sensitive personal information. Please do not send passwords, financial account details, government identifiers, or sensitive personal information to Bot commands or support channels.


## 2. How information is used
Information may be used to:
- provide Bot commands and features;
- store server settings and user progress so features work across sessions;
- execute moderation, role, welcome, ticket, and utility functions requested by authorized users;
- maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Bot;
- investigate abuse, errors, or violations of the Terms of Service; and
- respond to support requests.


## 3. Storage and retention
The Bot may store feature configuration and user-related records in a database so features persist. Data is retained only as long as needed for the feature, operational purposes, or other legitimate requirements, subject to the actual hosting and database configuration. Data may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion from active storage.

## 4. Sharing and service providers
We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure or service providers needed to run the Bot (such as database hosting, application hosting, or music-related services), subject to their own policies and configurations. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or others.

**Confirm the deployed providers and add their names/links here before publishing.**


## 5. Discord permissions and access
The Bot operates within Discord and may receive information available through the permissions, intents, and features enabled for it. Server administrators decide whether to add the Bot and what permissions to grant. Avoid granting permissions that are not needed.


## 6. Your choices and data requests
You can stop using the Bot or remove it from your server. For requests to access, correct, or delete information associated with your Discord ID or server, contact the operator using the support/contact method below. We may need to verify a request and may retain some information where legally required or necessary for security, dispute resolution, or legitimate operations.


## 7. Children's privacy
The Bot is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact the operator so the issue can be reviewed.


## 8. Security
Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect information, but no system, network, or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

This Policy may be updated as the Bot changes. The revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Check this page periodically.
